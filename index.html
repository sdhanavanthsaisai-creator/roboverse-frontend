03 — Problem statement & solution engine

Navigation under dynamic constraints.

RB/25 has to cross a 300 × 300 cm maze it has never seen while carrying up to 3 kg — and the maze refuses to sit still. Routes seal mid-drive; obstacles appear without notice. The drivetrain is a two-wheel differential drive: two 98 mm rubber traction wheels plus a castor, deliberately chosen over omni wheels so wheel-slip never corrupts the encoder measurement. Sensing fuses three ultrasonic rangefinders (left, front, right) with wheel-encoder odometry and an MPU-6050 IMU — the walls say where to turn, the encoders say how far it has moved, the gyro says how much it has turned. Run 1 hugs the left wall and records every move; Run 2 replays the shortened path at higher speed. We never touch a joystick.

No single sensor is enough on its own — together they let the rover choose a move, carry it out, and check the result.

EQ 01 — Drive · top speed

98 mm wheels at a rated 430 rpm

430 rpm is 7.17 rev/s over a 307.9 mm circumference — theory caps at 2.21 m/s, but inside 50 cm cells the rover needs accurate stops and turns, so Run 1 is driven much slower.

EQ 02 — Pull · grip check

29.2 N against the 5 kg assumption

Both motors pull about 60% of the assumed 5 kg robot-plus-payload weight, under the 0.7-grip limit — the wheels should not slip. Re-checked once the real mass is weighed.

EQ 03 — Odometry · ticks per cell

1.62 wheel turns per 50 cm cell

Ticks per cell is 1.62 × N, where N is encoder counts per turn (TBC) — calibrated by pushing the rover one cell by hand and reading the counts.

EQ 04 — Solver · path shortening

Dead ends cancel out

Counting left −90°, right +90° and U-turn 180°, each U-turn detour sums to its shortcut — the same final heading with less distance travelled.

EQ 05 — Power · battery sizing

179.5 W worst case, about 8.1 A

Ten minutes needs 1.35 Ah (1.69 at 80% usable) at about 4.8 C — so the pack must be rated 5 C or more. Pack choice TBD; running current to be measured.
